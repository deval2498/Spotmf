# 🪙 Product Overview — Automated Crypto Investing Platform

## 🌐 Product Summary

Our platform empowers crypto investors to **automate their token accumulation strategies** using on-chain smart contracts.
Users connect their **EVM wallets**, approve token spend to our contracts, and let our backend **execute disciplined, rule-based buys** according to the strategy they choose.

The goal is simple:

> Make crypto investing consistent, emotion-free, and intelligent — just like Linear’s design philosophy, but for DeFi.

---

## 💼 Core Concept

Instead of timing the market manually, users predefine **investment strategies** that execute automatically:

1. **Simple SIP (Systematic Investment Plan)** — buy tokens periodically at fixed intervals.
2. **DMA Strategy (Dynamic Moving Average)** — buy tokens when prices fall below moving averages (50, 100, or 200 DMA), ensuring statistically sound entries.

All execution is handled by our backend triggers, while **users retain full on-chain control** of their assets.

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Connect Wallet**
   Users connect any EVM-compatible wallet (e.g., MetaMask).
   The platform fetches their token balances and investment preferences.

2. **Approve Tokens**
   Users approve our smart contract to use a limited token amount for strategy execution.

3. **Select Strategy**

   - _Simple SIP_: choose time interval (e.g., weekly, monthly) and amount.
   - _DMA Strategy_: choose which moving average to track (50/100/200 DMA), cooldown duration, and investment amount.

4. **Automated Execution**
   The backend monitors market data and executes buys automatically according to the chosen strategy.

5. **Transparency**
   Every execution is visible on-chain, with complete transaction traceability.
